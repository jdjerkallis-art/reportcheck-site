ReportCheck

How do you find out a client report went out broken?

ReportCheck is a small UK research project. We're looking at how marketing agencies find out when a scheduled Looker Studio report goes out broken or empty, and whether an automatic check would be worth having.

What's available now

Free research check

For one reporting cycle, we run up to three PDF reports you have already sent to clients through our checker. We send back a plain list of what it flagged and which pages it could not check.

  • It looks for visible error panels, missing chart areas and the wrong reporting period.
  • It does not check your numbers or how fresh the data is. Pages containing images, including logos, are listed as not checked.
  • It doesn't replace your own review.
  • No cost, no card, no auto-renewal and no obligation to buy anything.

What we're exploring

Not available yet

An automatic check that reviews each scheduled report shortly before it reaches the client, and warns you if something looks wrong. We haven't built it. If it would be useful to you, or if you already have this covered, we'd like to hear either way.

Your files

  • Share reports through a link you control and can revoke at any time. Please don't send attachments.
  • Only send reports you are free to share. Redact anything confidential if you're unsure.
  • We never ask for logins, passwords, API keys or access to your accounts.
  • Files are used only for your check and deleted within 14 days of us sending the result. They aren't shared or used to train anything.
  • We won't use your name or a case study without your written permission.
